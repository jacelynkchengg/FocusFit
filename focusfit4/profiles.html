Focus Profiles

Five focus styles, five different setups. Take the quiz to find yours, or browse them all here.

💤

Quiet Deep Worker

For people who need silence and minimal stimulation. Best in a library, a clean desk, or a quiet corner, working in long uninterrupted blocks.

☕️

Cafe Momentum Worker

For people who focus better around light background noise and other people working. Cafes, student centers, and study lounges pair well with short pomodoro-style sprints.

🏃‍♀️

Sprint Starter

For people who find starting the hardest part. Small, low-pressure sprints and a 2-minute start rule turn procrastination into momentum.

📋

Structured Planner

For people who focus best once everything is organized first. A clear task list, ranked priorities, and time blocking keep the day on track.

🎨

Creative Flow Worker

Best for design, brainstorming, writing, and other creative work. Flexible sessions, inspiration boards, and ambient music fuel the flow.
